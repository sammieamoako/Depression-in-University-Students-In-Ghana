## Depression in University Students in Ghana

### Overview

This project investigates the **prevalence and predictors of depression** among university students in Ghana. It focuses on identifying the factors that contribute to mental health challenges in this population. The study examines demographic characteristics, academic stress, work-related pressure, lifestyle habits, and family history of mental illness. Key variables such as **sleep duration, dietary habits, financial stress, and suicidal thoughts** are included to provide a comprehensive understanding of student mental health.

The primary goal is to uncover **risk factors for depression** and support the development of **targeted strategies for early intervention**, while ensuring the **ethical handling of sensitive mental health data**.

### Data Description

**Format:** CSV (each row represents a participating student)

**Variables may include:**

* **Demographics:** Age, Gender, Program/Degree
* **Academic Factors:** Academic Pressure, Study Satisfaction
* **Lifestyle Habits:** Sleep Duration, Dietary Habits
* **Stress Indicators:** Financial Stress, Work-Related Stress
* **Family & Psychological Factors:** Family History of Mental Illness, Suicidal Thoughts

**Target Variable:**

* Depression status, determined by reported emotional and behavioral indicators

### Key Definitions

* **Depression:** A state of persistent sadness, hopelessness, loss of interest, difficulty concentrating, sleep or appetite changes, and possibly suicidal thoughts.
* **Academic Pressure:** Stress from academic tasks such as exams, tests, or assignments.
* **Study Satisfaction:** The student’s level of contentment with their academic experience.
* **Degree:** The academic program or qualification the student is currently pursuing.

### Applications

* Identify major predictors of depression in Ghanaian university students
* Analyze mental health trends and high-risk groups
* Support development of intervention and counseling strategies
* Provide data for research in mental health, education, and policy planning

### Ethical Considerations

All data is anonymized, and sensitive information is managed with confidentiality. Users are expected to respect ethical standards when analyzing, interpreting, or sharing results based on the dataset.
